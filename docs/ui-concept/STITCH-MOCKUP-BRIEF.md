# Orbit Social Agent — Google Stitch Mockup Brief

## Product concept

Design a desktop-first SaaS application called **Orbit**, an AI social media manager for founders, local businesses, and small agencies. Orbit learns a company’s Brand DNA, generates daily content ideas, turns selected ideas into finished multi-platform campaigns, routes sensitive content for approval, schedules and publishes posts, and learns from performance.

This is not primarily a chatbot and not a traditional analytics dashboard. The main experience is a visual creative workspace supported by a proactive agent.

Use the rules in `DESIGN.md` as the visual source of truth.

## Generate these six connected screens

### Screen 1 — Today / Agent briefing

Create a desktop application shell with a dark charcoal left navigation and a warm off-white workspace. The selected brand is **Luna Brew Cafe**.

The workspace begins with a concise morning briefing: “Good morning. Your launch campaign is on track.” Below it, show three prioritized actions as a structured list, not equal cards:

- Two Instagram concepts need approval.
- Thursday has an open publishing slot.
- The “behind the bar” video format is outperforming static product posts.

The dominant module is “Today’s proposed plan,” a timeline of what the agent intends to create, revise, and publish. Add a compact approval strip with visual thumbnails. A right-side agent activity rail explains current work in plain language and shows an emergency “Pause autopilot” control.

### Screen 2 — Ideas feed

Create an editorial media grid titled “Ideas for Luna Brew.” Include filter tabs for All, Organic, Promotions, Education, Community, and Experiments. Use varied but aligned 4:5 and square image/video concept previews.

Each idea includes a short concept title, intended platform, content purpose, and tiny reason such as “Based on customer questions.” Hover state exposes Save, Skip, and Variations. One selected idea opens a right inspector containing the hook, rationale, suggested format, target audience, and buttons for “Build this” and “Save for later.”

Make this feel faster and more visual than a form-based generator.

### Screen 3 — Create campaign

Build a focused source-to-campaign screen. The main input is a large paper-like composer with the prompt: “What should Orbit turn into a campaign?” It supports a URL, pasted text, uploaded product images, or a short brief.

Below it, include compact controls for campaign goal, offer, target platforms, publish window, and approval mode. On the right, show Brand DNA being applied: Warm and polished voice, nearby professionals audience, banned claims, and visual references. One Coral primary action says “Build campaign.”

Do not use a chat transcript.

### Screen 4 — Creative workspace

Show the campaign **Summer Cold Brew Flight**. Use a 65/35 layout. The left side contains a large editable Instagram creative preview with direct text editing and a filmstrip of four visual variants below it. Above the canvas are format controls for Feed post, Story, Reel, and Carousel.

The right inspector contains four tabs: Copy, Design, Adaptations, and Checks. The Checks tab shows brand voice passed, price claim needs approval, safe-area passed, and Instagram caption length passed. Include actions for Regenerate, Request a change, and Approve.

At the bottom, show compact platform adaptations for Facebook, Google Business, LinkedIn, and TikTok as a structured list with thumbnail, status, and last-edited time—not equal cards.

### Screen 5 — Calendar and publishing

Create a weekly content calendar with media thumbnails placed in time slots. Include an unscheduled tray on the left and a publishing-health rail on the right. Each scheduled item shows channel, format, approval state, and whether Orbit or a human scheduled it.

Show one failed Google Business publish attempt with a clear inline explanation and Retry action. Show one future open slot recommended by the agent. Include filters for brand and channel, plus a timezone indicator for Asia/Manila.

### Screen 6 — Brand DNA

Create a brand intelligence workspace rather than a settings form. Use sections for Voice, Audience, Offers, Visual world, Proof and claims, Restricted language, Content pillars, and Connected sources.

Show extracted website evidence beside editable agent conclusions. Include visual reference thumbnails, approved example posts, and a confidence marker for inferred information. Add an activity note: “Orbit updated the audience profile after five approved edits.” Every inferred value must be reviewable and reversible.

## Persistent interaction model

- The left navigation includes Today, Ideas, Create, Campaigns, Calendar, Insights, and Brand DNA.
- A global “Create” action is always available but visually restrained.
- The agent communicates through briefings, recommendations, rationales, and activity status—not constant chat bubbles.
- Approval-sensitive content always explains why review is required.
- Generated content is never presented as automatically published unless its status explicitly says so.
- Mobile versions collapse to a single work column, a five-item bottom bar, and inspector bottom sheets.

## Visual direction

Warm editorial creative studio meets dependable operations software. Use Satoshi and Geist Mono, a warm paper canvas, charcoal navigation, and one muted Coral accent. Generated media provides color. Avoid purple AI styling, glassmorphism, neon, giant gradient headings, uniform card grids, and generic dashboard KPI rows.
