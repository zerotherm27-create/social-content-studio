# Properties by Chel Content Strategy

## Purpose

Properties by Chel should function as Chel's personal authority and relationship brand. It should help people understand who Chel is, why they should trust her with real estate decisions, and how they can work with her.

The brand should feel warmer and more personal than Allabode Realty and Appraisal Services. It should not compete with Allabode as a full service company website. Instead, it should build trust, educate the market, and guide serious inquiries toward Allabode.

## Recommended Brand Role

Properties by Chel should be positioned as the personal real estate advisory brand of Chel, a licensed Real Estate Broker and Appraiser in the Philippines.

Recommended positioning statement:

> Practical real estate guidance from a licensed broker and appraiser, helping buyers, sellers, property owners, tenants, and investors make clearer property decisions in the Philippines.

Relationship to Allabode:

> Properties by Chel is Chel's personal real estate advisory and content brand. For full-service brokerage, leasing, property management, and appraisal, clients are served through Allabode Realty and Appraisal Services.

## Website Direction

The website should be simple, personal, and credibility-led. It should not try to become a large property marketplace. Its job is to make people trust Chel and then move qualified leads into the right service path.

Recommended pages:

1. Home
2. About Chel
3. Work With Me
4. Property Guides
5. Testimonials or Client Stories
6. Contact or Book a Consultation

- Experience and service focus
- Personal approach to clients
- Values such as transparency, practical advice, professionalism, and care
- Connection to Allabode as the formal service company

Suggested paragraph:

> Properties by Chel is my personal real estate advisory brand. I created it to share practical property guidance and help clients make informed decisions. For full-service brokerage, leasing, property management, and appraisal, I serve clients through Allabode Realty and Appraisal Services.

## Social Media Content Pillars

Properties by Chel should be the social and educational voice of the business. It should make Chel visible, helpful, and trustworthy before someone becomes a client.

### Property Education

Simple tips for buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, and investors.

Example topics:

- Mistakes sellers make when pricing property
- Questions tenants should ask before signing a lease
- What buyers should check before making an offer

### Appraisal Insights

Explain appraisal concepts in plain language.

Example topics:

- What affects property value?
- Difference between market price and appraised value
- Why location, condition, and comparables matter

### Owner And Landlord Tips

Practical content for property owners who want better occupancy, smoother leasing, and fewer management issues.

Example topics:

- Tenant screening basics
- Preparing a unit for rent
- Short-term vs long-term rental tradeoffs

### Market Commentary

Chel's practical point of view on property trends, rental demand, buyer behavior, and investment risk.

Example topics:

- What owners should watch in the current rental market
- Why some units lease faster than others
- How buyers can think about value instead of hype

### Behind The Brand

Personal, trust-building content from Chel's work.

Example topics:

- Site visits
- Appraisal fieldwork
- Client lessons
- Property preparation
- Day-in-the-life real estate work

## Lead Flow

The brand should guide visitors into the right next step:

- Personal advice: book a consultation with Chel
- Leasing, selling, property management, or appraisal service: go to Allabode
- Appraisal technology or data product interest: join the future PropIntel waitlist

Recommended footer language:

> Properties by Chel is the personal real estate advisory brand of Chel, founder of Allabode Realty and Appraisal Services.

## 30-Day Content Starter Plan

Week 1:

- Introduce Chel and her professional background
- Explain the difference between a broker and an appraiser
- Share a leasing checklist for property owners

Week 2:

- Post a guide on pricing before selling
- Share a short video on what affects property value
- Explain when owners should consider property management

Week 3:

- Share a tenant screening tip
- Post a buyer warning about overpaying
- Share a behind-the-scenes site visit or appraisal workflow

Week 4:

- Publish an OFW property management guide
- Share a short client-safe success story
- Invite people to book a consultation or visit Allabode

## Final Recommendation

Keep Properties by Chel active, but make its role clear. It should be the personal trust and education layer. Allabode should be the company that delivers full-service real estate, leasing, property management, brokerage, and appraisal. PropIntel should remain separate as the future appraisal technology product.

The clearest structure is:

- Properties by Chel builds trust.
- Allabode converts and serves clients.
- PropIntel scales appraisal intelligence later.
