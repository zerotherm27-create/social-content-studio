## 1. Company Information

**Business name:** All Abode Property Solutions  
**Official tagline:** Complete Property Services. One Trusted Partner.  
**Domain:** allabodeph.com  
**Country:** Philippines  
**Industry:** Real estate and property services  

**Service descriptor:**

> Brokerage | Leasing | Property Management | Appraisal

The founder is a licensed Real Estate Broker and Real Estate Appraiser in the Philippines.

All Abode Property Solutions should be positioned as a professional, trustworthy, full-service property company serving property owners, investors, buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, and appraisal clients.

The company should not be positioned as only a property listing website or only a traditional brokerage. Its main distinction is the ability to support clients across the property lifecycle through brokerage, leasing, management, and appraisal expertise.
